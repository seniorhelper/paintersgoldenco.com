Before you fall in love with a color

Sample it on the actual wall. A two-foot square on the real surface, looked at in morning, midday and evening light, tells you more than an hour with a fan deck. Colors read lighter and cooler outdoors here, and what looks rich in the store often washes out on a sunny Golden elevation.

If your home is in a historic district or a covenant community, check the approved palette before sampling. We will pull the list and handle the submission.

Straight answers

Color questions from Golden homeowners

How do I pick exterior paint colors in Golden?

Sample on the actual wall, not on a chip indoors. Paint a two-foot square, then look at it at midday and again at dusk. Golden's elevation and western exposure make colors read lighter and cooler outdoors than they do in a store.

What paint brands do you use?

All major brands, including Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr, Valspar and PPG. The exact product line and sheen are named on your written estimate.

Do colors fade faster at Golden's elevation?

Yes, and saturated colors fade fastest. Deep reds, bright blues and strong greens lose intensity sooner than mid-tones under high-altitude ultraviolet exposure, especially on south and west walls.

Can I use any color in the historic district?

Not automatically. Exterior color changes on landmarked and contributing properties in Golden go through historic preservation review before work begins. We help prepare the submission.

What about HOA communities like Genesee?

Most covenant communities publish an approved palette. Start there, and we will pull the list and prepare the submission before scheduling.

Will you bring samples?

Yes. We put samples on your actual surfaces and look at them in the light your house actually gets, rather than asking you to decide from a fan deck.
