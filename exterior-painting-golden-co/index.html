The canyon is the local variable

Most Front Range exterior advice is written for open suburban lots. Golden is not that. The city sits in the gap where Clear Creek comes out of the foothills, hemmed by North and South Table Mountain on one side and the hogback on the other, and that shapes the work.

  • Channeled wind. Downslope air accelerates coming out of the canyon. It carries grit, it skins a coating faster than the manufacturer intended, and on a bad afternoon it ends painting for the day.
  • West-facing walls take the worst of it. Wind-driven abrasion plus full afternoon ultraviolet on the same elevation. Those walls commonly need attention a cycle before the rest of the house.
  • Elevation raises the ultraviolet load. Golden sits meaningfully above Denver, and the foothills properties above it higher still. Thinner atmosphere filters less, and the binder in the coating is what pays for it.
  • Shorter usable season. The practical window is mid-May through early October, and tighter as you climb.

Season, honestly

Mid-May to early October in town. Denver-area climate records put the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the average first fall freeze near October 7, and Golden's elevation tightens both ends. Up on Lookout Mountain and toward Genesee it is tighter again.

The number that governs a given day is the overnight low rather than the daytime high, because a coating applied at a pleasant 65 degrees still has to cure through the night. We will move a date rather than paint into a bad window.

If your house is in a historic district

A color change on an exterior in the 8th and 9th Street, 12th Street or East Street districts generally needs a Certificate of Appropriateness before work begins, and the board meets monthly. Build that into your plan early. Our timeline planner works the dates out.

What is in the scope

  1. Protect plants, walks, drives, windows, fixtures and vehicles.
  2. Pressure wash the whole exterior and allow full dry time.
  3. Treat mildew on shaded elevations before coating.
  4. Scrape every loose edge back to sound material.
  5. Sand transitions so the old line does not telegraph through.
  6. Fill, then caulk every joint, seam and penetration.
  7. Prime all bare substrate and every repair.
  8. Two finish coats on siding, trim, fascia, soffit and doors as scoped.
  9. Full cleanup, then a walkthrough you lead before the balance is due.

Questions

Exterior painting questions

When can you paint the outside of my house in Golden?

Generally mid-May through early October. Denver-area records place the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the first fall freeze near October 7, and Golden's elevation tightens both ends. Foothills properties have a shorter window again.

How much does exterior painting cost in Golden?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface. Condition of the existing coating and any substrate repair move a quote far more than square footage does.

Why is my west wall so much worse than the others?

Two forces on the same elevation. Downslope wind out of Clear Creek Canyon carries grit and abrades the film, and the same wall takes hours of direct afternoon ultraviolet. Doing that elevation alone, sooner, is often the sensible spend.

Does wind actually affect the painting?

Yes. Channeled canyon wind skins a coating faster than the manufacturer intended and drives grit into a wet film. On a bad afternoon it ends painting for the day, which is why Golden schedules need realistic contingency.

Do I need approval before changing my exterior color?

If your property is in one of Golden's three historic districts or is individually designated, a color change generally requires a Certificate of Appropriateness. The board meets monthly and wants applications weeks ahead, so plan early.

Do you pressure wash first?

Always. Paint will not bond to chalk, dust or mildew, and Golden's sun-and-wind-exposed walls chalk heavily. Washing is part of the scope, not an add-on.
