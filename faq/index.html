Historic districts and approvals

Do I need a Certificate of Appropriateness to paint my house?

If your property is in the 8th and 9th Street, 12th Street or East Street historic district, or is individually designated, most exterior alterations require COA review. Repainting in the existing colors is generally maintenance rather than an alteration.

When does the Historic Preservation Board meet?

The second Wednesday of each month. Complete applications are generally due at least 4 to 6 weeks before the meeting date, and complex projects should involve a conversation with city staff first.

What does a COA cost?

A Certificate of Appropriateness for Compatibility, which covers alterations, carries no fee according to the city. The $500 fee applies to demolition applications, a separate category.

Which districts are designated in Golden?

Three: the 8th and 9th Street District, the 12th Street District and the East Street District. The program began in 1983 with the 12th Street designation, and individually designated sites exist elsewhere in the city.

My house is old but I do not think it is in a district.

Check with the city anyway. A building over 50 years old might be designated or part of a district, and individually designated sites sit outside the district boundaries.

Will you prepare the application?

Yes, as part of the project: the letter of intent, elevation drawings specifying materials with manufacturers and colors, and documentation of the existing structure.

Hiring and licensing

Does Colorado license painting contractors?

No statewide painting license exists. Some municipalities have their own requirements. Current liability insurance, a detailed written scope and a readable warranty are what actually protect you.

Does Golden require a contractor license?

Golden requires a contractor license for work within city limits that requires a building permit, at $100 per trade. Painting alone does not normally trigger a permit, though associated structural or siding work can.

How do I compare bids?

On four lines rather than the total: the preparation included, the exact product and sheen, the coat count, and the warranty terms. A lower number is nearly always one of those being smaller.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully, with the certificate provided at the estimate rather than on request afterward.

The work and the season

When can you paint my exterior?

Mid-May through early October in town. Denver-area records place the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the first fall freeze near October 7, and Golden's elevation tightens both ends. Foothills properties run roughly mid-June through September.

Can you paint inside during winter?

Yes, and it is the better use of the calendar here. Interior work happens inside a heated house, and Golden's exterior season is short enough that winter interiors make sense.

What preparation is included?

Protection of everything not being painted, washing, mildew treatment where needed, scraping to a sound edge, sanding transitions, filling and caulking, and priming all bare or repaired substrate. Each step is itemized by surface in your estimate.

Which products do you use?

Sherwin-Williams and PPG, with the exact line and sheen named per surface so you can check the manufacturer's data sheet.

Does the canyon wind really affect the work?

Yes. Downslope air out of Clear Creek Canyon skins a coating faster than intended and drives grit into a wet film. On a bad afternoon it ends painting for the day, so Golden schedules carry realistic contingency.

How long will my house take?

A straightforward Golden exterior is often three to five days. A period home with extensive preparation runs longer, and your estimate states the real schedule rather than an optimistic one.

Older homes and special surfaces

My walls are plaster. Does that change interior work?

Yes. Plaster can key off, separating from the lath behind while looking sound from the front. We tap and check before quoting, because painting a section that has keyed off puts a finish on a failing surface.

What changes on a pre-1978 house?

Lead-safe work practices. Federal EPA rules require certified firms plus containment, defined work methods and verified cleanup when disturbing painted surfaces in housing built before 1978.

Can you paint brick?

Yes, with a conversation first. It is close to irreversible and needs a breathable masonry coating plus good moisture management. On a designated property it is also a question for the board.

Does stucco need special treatment?

Usually. Stucco is porous and generally needs to breathe, so coatings are chosen for vapor permeability. A product that seals the wall can trap moisture and push itself off, taking surface stucco with it.

Do you work on log homes?

Yes, throughout the foothills. Log and heavy-timber surfaces need penetrating stain systems plus attention to checking and chinking.

Money and guarantees

How much will my project cost?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exteriors at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot and interiors at about $1.50 to $3.50. In Golden the age of the coatings and the complexity of the trim move it most.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Free from photos or in person, with no obligation.

When do I pay?

A deposit holds your place on the schedule; the balance follows the walkthrough, once you have looked at everything and signed off. Both figures are printed on the estimate rather than discussed later.

What does the 5-year warranty cover?

Our workmanship: the preparation and the application. If our work fails within five years we return and correct it. The coating carries its own manufacturer warranty alongside ours.

Is fading covered?

No, and no honest painter covers it. Ultraviolet degrades pigment over years, which at Golden's elevation is faster than on the plain. That is why product grade matters here.
